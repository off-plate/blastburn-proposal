Blast & Burn Gym, Durrës

Design system

Built before any screen was designed, and rebuilt once after the first review rejected it. Every colour below is sampled from photographs of their actual gym. If a page needs something that is not on this page, it gets added here first.

1 · Provenance

The logo carries no brand colour. Sampling logo-gym-copy.png returns #F8F8F8 (80,092 px), #101010 (4,943 px) and one desaturated blue-grey #607080 (1,823 px). So the palette cannot honestly come from the logo. It comes from the room, photographed 35 times and measured. Each source is shown beside the value it produced, so the claim can be checked rather than believed.

SourceWhere it is in the gymMeasuredBecomes
Walls and ceiling, throughout #000000 #101010 #141414 #101014 --void --surface
Painted wall type: "NO PAIN - NO POWER", "BEAST MODE: ON" #AE1212 to #EA4230, centre #C9281F --red --red-lift
OSB columns and blonde floor #9C8A66 #A2906C #A89672 --osb
The hand-painted Goku mural, roughly three metres wide #182466 #121866 #1E2A6C --mural
The branching LED tubes on the ceiling #FFFFFF --led and the signature

Both reds are measured values, not interpolations. --red #C9281F is the centre of the sampled range. --red-lift #EA4230 is the same paint where the LED bars hit it, and it is the cut that clears AA for small text at 4.98:1. Nothing was nudged for taste.

The risk, stated plainly. "Near-black ground with one hot accent" is listed in design-log.md as a 2026 AI default and is off the table by default. The first review of this page rejected the defence, correctly: the tan was argued for in prose and then never used as a surface. It is now load-bearing. --osb is a full-bleed band carrying dark type, and the section divider is permanently tan. If the tan came out, the system would visibly break, which is the only test of a co-lead that means anything.

5 · The signature

Their ceiling is hung with branching LED tubes: straight runs joined at angles with arms splitting off. It is the one thing in that room you could not mistake for another gym, so it is drawn at architectural scale and crosses the canvas. It is not a 4px divider rule. One per screenful, never inside a card.

the same fixture, small, as a lockup mark
