Training

What runs on this floor

Six disciplines, documented like a spec sheet. Format, and what it actually is.

01CrossFit Group
Functional strength and conditioning, coached rep by rep. A first session and a fifth year run side by side, scaled to whoever turns up.
02HIIT Group, 45 min
Short rounds at full effort against measured rest. The class that fits a lunch hour.
03Aerobics Group, 45 min to 1 hr
Music-led cardiovascular work. Their own site calls it beginner friendly, and the sessions run longer than the other group classes.
04Abs and core Group, 30 min
Direct trunk work that holds up everything else you lift. Short enough to add onto another class.
05Personal training One to one
One coach, one plan, technique corrected as it happens. Their trainers are certified. Booked and paid separately from a membership.
06Posture and rehabilitation One to one, by appointment
Listed on their Instagram but not on their website, which means it is a real service with no page selling it yet. For a desk back, or a joint coming back from injury. Assessed before it is programmed.

Pick one

There is no published timetable. Call and say which discipline; they tell you when it next runs.
