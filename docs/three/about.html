The room

Every gym says the same thing about itself. Blast & Burn is on Rruga Glaukia 17 in Durrës, next to the Ibrahim Kodra school, and it is open thirteen hours a day, every day the Google listing shows. That is the part that is checkable. The rest is what its own site and its own coaches say about it, and it is worth reading with that in mind.

“Everyone needs to do something physical, in whatever form suits them.”— from their own site

That is their own framing, translated from Albanian, and it is a fairer pitch than most gyms make. The floor runs six things: CrossFit, HIIT, aerobics, core work, personal training and posture and rehabilitation, the last of which their website has never actually sold, only their Instagram has. Personal training and posture and rehabilitation are both one to one with a certified trainer, booked separately from a membership.

What is actually true
  • 5.0 on Google, from 113 reviews, verified 10 Aug 2026
  • 9,711 following on Instagram, same date
  • Their own site lists a women's training area, separate from the main floor, alongside the shared showers.
  • A towel and a pair of indoor trainers, in their own words.

There is a 20% discount for signing up now on their own about page. It is carried over here as theirs, not invented, and it is the kind of thing that needs confirming still runs before anyone is quoted it.
