Questions

Do you open at weekends?

Their site says Monday to Saturday, 10:00 to 22:00. Google lists 09:00 to 22:00 every day. The two disagree, so this is the answer to confirm before the site goes live.

What do I need to bring?

A towel and a pair of indoor trainers. That is their own answer, on their own contact page.

Are there showers?

Yes, separate for men and women.

Do you work with personal trainers?

Yes, certified ones. Sessions are booked separately.

Can I train without joining a class?

Yes. Every membership opens the full floor for all thirteen hours it is open.

Can I try it before I pay?

Call ahead on a first visit. Somebody meets you at the door and walks the floor with you.
