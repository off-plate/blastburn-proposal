Questions

Are you open every day?
Google shows 09:00 to 22:00, seven days. Their own website states Monday to Saturday, 10:00 to 22:00. The two disagree, so this is confirmed with the gym before the real site goes live, not guessed at.
What should I bring on the first visit?
A towel and a pair of indoor trainers. Their own contact page gives this exact answer.
Are there showers?
Yes, kept separate for men and women.
Is there a women's training area?
Yes. Their own site mentions a women's area separate from the main floor.
Do you work with personal trainers?
Yes, and they are certified.
Do I need to join a class, or can I just train?
Either. A membership opens the whole floor for all thirteen hours it is open, with or without a class.
