The room keeps the receipts

Blast & Burn is a gym on Rruga Glaukia in Durrës. It opens at nine and shuts at ten, seven days a week, and somebody who knows what they are looking at is on the floor for all thirteen hours.

The rates The index

What it is

A single floor carrying free weights, machines and a class studio. Six disciplines run out of it, four in groups and two one to one. Nothing is booked through an app, because the person you would be booking is standing there.

How it works

You are assessed before you are programmed. The plan is written down rather than remembered, reviewed on a fixed date, and changed when the numbers rather than the mood say it should be.

What it costs

One rate opens the whole floor and every group class. One to one work is charged by the session. The full list is set out below, and there is nothing hidden underneath it.
